## P1 item 6: fundamentals as strategy inputs (rows N34 to N39)

**Date:** 2026-11-07. Built and passing in automated tests: the brief's fixture back tested from 2019 on 30 made-up companies with quarterly filings (every yearly selection matches a hand computation from filings accepted before the rebalance, a filing accepted 30 minutes after a rebalance close is not used, and no company with negative trailing EPS is ever selected), unit tests of the filing replay (TTM, year-to-date quarters, restatements, splits, acceptance times), worker tests of the sync, and a browser run of "dividend yield > 3%" saved, shown, back tested and matched by the screener. All against a local stand-in for SEC EDGAR: the cloud build machine can't reach sec.gov.

| #   | Point                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Brief                                            | Recommend                                                                                                                                                                              |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| O19 | **The one exception to "missing data is never zero" (cited by N35 and ADR 0043): EV/EBITDA counts missing debt, preferred stock and minority interest as zero.** Most companies have no preferred stock or minority interest and so report no line for them; some have no debt. Treating an unreported line as unknown (principle 4) would make EV/EBITDA unknown for most of the S&P 500. Cash is required (no cash line, no EV/EBITDA). | "Missing data is never zero"                     | Keep: an absent balance-sheet line is a zero balance, not missing data. Alternative: unknown unless debt is reported.                                                                  |
| O20 | **The screener assumes no stock split since the latest filing.** Strategies correct per-share figures and share counts for splits using raw and split-adjusted price history; the screener has only the latest price, so for a stock that split since its last 10-Q, the screener's P/E and yield are off until the next filing (a few weeks at most).                                                                                    | Screener parity                                  | Keep for now; the screener can read split-adjusted history later if it matters.                                                                                                        |
| O21 | **Dividend-growth streak counts fiscal years, not calendar years.** It is read from each 10-K's own this-year and prior-year dividends per share (so a split can't break it), which are fiscal years; most S&P companies' fiscal year is the calendar year.                                                                                                                                                                               | "Consecutive calendar years"                     | Keep fiscal years.                                                                                                                                                                     |

Coverage across the real S&P 500 is not yet measured.                                                                                                                    | "If EDGAR cannot support this reliably, flag it" | Measure coverage on the Mac with real filings (screener column filled vs. known payers) before relying on it; if it is poor, keep the metric unimplemented for those companies as now. |
| O23 | **EBITDA is always operating income + depreciation and amortization.** US GAAP filings have no standard EBITDA tag, so "as reported" is not available from XBRL.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | "As reported, or OI + D&A"                       | Keep.                                                                                                                                                                                  |
