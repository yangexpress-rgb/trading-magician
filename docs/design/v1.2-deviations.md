## P1 item 6: fundamentals as strategy inputs (rows N34 to N39)

**Date:** 2026-11-07. Built and passing in automated tests: the brief's fixture back tested from 2019 on 30 made-up companies with quarterly filings (every yearly selection matches a hand computation from filings accepted before the rebalance, a filing accepted 30 minutes after a rebalance close is not used, and no company with negative trailing EPS is ever selected), unit tests of the filing replay (TTM, year-to-date quarters, restatements, splits, acceptance times), worker tests of the sync, and a browser run of "dividend yield > 3%" saved, shown, back tested and matched by the screener. All against a local stand-in for SEC EDGAR: the cloud build machine can't reach sec.gov.

| #   | Point                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Brief                                            | Recommend                                                                                                                                                                              |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| O19 | **The one exception to "missing data is never zero" (cited by N35 and ADR 0043): EV/EBITDA counts missing debt, preferred stock and minority interest as zero.** Most companies have no preferred stock or minority interest and so report no line for them; some have no debt. Treating an unreported line as unknown (principle 4) would make EV/EBITDA unknown for most of the S&P 500. Cash is required (no cash line, no EV/EBITDA). | "Missing data is never zero"                     | Keep: an absent balance-sheet line is a zero balance, not missing data. Alternative: unknown unless debt is reported.                                                                  |
| O20 | **The screener assumes no stock split since the latest filing.** Strategies correct per-share figures and share counts for splits using raw and split-adjusted price history; the screener has only the latest price, so for a stock that split since its last 10-Q, the screener's P/E and yield are off until the next filing (a few weeks at most).                                                                                    | Screener parity                                  | Keep for now; the screener can read split-adjusted history later if it matters.                                                                                                        |
| O21 | **Dividend-growth streak counts fiscal years, not calendar years.** It is read from each 10-K's own this-year and prior-year dividends per share (so a split can't break it), which are fiscal years; most S&P companies' fiscal year is the calendar year.                                                                                                                                                                               | "Consecutive calendar years"                     | Keep fiscal years.                                                                                                                                                                     |
| O22 | **Dividend yield uses dividends declared per share as tagged in 10-K/10-Q filings** (`CommonStockDividendsPerShareDeclared`, else `…CashPaid`). A company that pays dividends but does not tag them per share has no dividend yield (unknown, not approximated). Coverage across the real S&P 500 is not yet measured.                                                                                                                    | "If EDGAR cannot support this reliably, flag it" | Measure coverage on the Mac with real filings (screener column filled vs. known payers) before relying on it; if it is poor, keep the metric unimplemented for those companies as now. |
| O23 | **EBITDA is always operating income + depreciation and amortization.** US GAAP filings have no standard EBITDA tag, so "as reported" is not available from XBRL.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | "As reported, or OI + D&A"                       | Keep.                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| O24 | **Filings are fetched on demand and refreshed after a day.** The first S&P 500 back test waits about two minutes for ~1,000 SEC requests; a screen loads for up to a minute and fills the rest on later screens. Values appear at the 10-Q/10-K acceptance, not at the earlier earnings press release (8-K), so back tests see them a few days later than the market did (never earlier).                                                 | —                                                | Keep; add a nightly background sync of S&P 500 members if the first wait bothers.                                                                                                      |

## P2 item 9: risk-based sizing and an ATR stop (row N42)

**Date:** 2026-11-07. Built and passing in automated tests: the brief's fixture (SPY trend entry, 1% risk with 2 × ATR(20), ATR stop at 2 × ATR(20)) with three trades' quantities recomputed by hand from the equity and ATR the trade list shows, the ATR stop firing with no condition exit and labelled, the cap at the maximum order size, no entry without an ATR, paper sizing (including the "rounds to 0" skip; the paper stop shares the back test's level and check functions but has no paper test of its own), and a browser run from picking the sizing in the builder to the trade list. Fixed-quantity strategies back test as before.

| #   | Point                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Brief                                                                      | Recommend                                                                                              |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| O28 | **Non-trailing only.** The ATR stop's level is fixed at entry; there is no `trailing` option yet. A trailing stop needs its own ratchet rules (which ATR, which extreme, close or high) written down first; the brief allows shipping without it.                                                                                     | "If trailing complicates this round, ship non-trailing only and say so"    | Keep; build trailing later from a written rule if wanted.                                              |
| O29 | **One ATR stop per Exit, watching the first Entry leg.** A strategy whose Entry trades several instruments gets one stop level, from the first leg's instrument, and closes every leg when it fires; validation warns about this. Per-leg stops would need per-leg exits, which Exit does not have.                                   | "Per-position state: entry price and ATR at entry"                         | Confirm; per-leg stops can follow if multi-leg strategies need them. **Confirmed by Yong 2026-11-07.** |
| O30 | **Daily ATR, whatever the run's interval, and close-based on daily closes.** Both the sizing ATR and the stop read daily bars (an hourly back test still checks the stop once a day, at the close). On paper the stop compares the latest daily close with the level, so an intraday plunge that recovers by the close does not exit. | "Close-based, consistent with the engine's other exits; no intrabar fills" | Confirm. **Confirmed by Yong 2026-11-07.**                                                             |
