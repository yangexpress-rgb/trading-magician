Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.

On March 4, 2025, Big Buyer Corp., a Delaware corporation (the “Company”), entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with Small Target Inc., a Nevada corporation (“Target”), and Buyer Sub LLC (“Merger Sub”). Merger Sub will merge with and into Small Target Inc., with Small Target Inc. surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company. Each share of Target common stock will be converted into the right to receive $12.25 in cash.
